Human Resources Policy — CONFIDENTIAL

Salary Bands (FY2025):
- Level 1 (Junior): $65,000 – $85,000
- Level 2 (Mid): $85,000 – $120,000
- Level 3 (Senior): $120,000 – $165,000
- Level 4 (Staff): $165,000 – $220,000
- Level 5 (Principal): $220,000 – $300,000

Layoff Policy: In the event of workforce reduction, employees will receive
2 weeks of severance per year of service. Health insurance coverage continues
for 90 days post-termination.

Performance Review Cycle: Annual reviews are conducted in Q4. Ratings of
"Below Expectations" for two consecutive cycles result in a PIP
(Performance Improvement Plan) with a 60-day remediation window.
